## What was wrong and what the change does

`get_rounding_mode()` of `cbrt.c`, `rsqrt.c` and `asinpi.c` (CORE-MATH), under
`#if defined(__x86_64__)`, turns the rounding field of MXCSR into the `FE_*`
value of the C library: `>>5` (or a table) for the UCRT under
`#if defined(__WIN32__) || defined(__WIN64__)`, `>>3` (glibc values)
otherwise. clang-cl defines `__x86_64__`, `_WIN32`, `_WIN64`, `_MSC_VER`,
`_M_X64` but neither `__WIN32__` nor `__WIN64__` (checked with `clang-18
--target=x86_64-pc-windows-msvc -dM -E` and `clang-cl-18 /E -Xclang -dM`), and
uses the UCRT's `fenv.h` (`FE_DOWNWARD` 0x100, `FE_UPWARD` 0x200,
`FE_TOWARDZERO` 0x300): the three computed 0x400/0x800/0xc00 and compared them
with 0x100/0x200/0x300, so the directed roundings were taken for toward zero
(the clang-cl object of `cbrt.c` even folds it to `testl $0x6000` → 3).

The change, as decided by the maintainer: one token in each of the three
files, marked like the other GAOL changes of the vendored sources:

```c
  #if defined(__WIN32__) || defined(__WIN64__) || defined(_WIN32) /* GAOL */
```

clang-cl then takes the Windows branch (`FE_UPWARD == 0x0200` → `>>5`; the
table for the pre-14393 UCRT). The `cbrt.c` change of #54 (`mode =
fegetround(); /* GAOL: ... */`) is untouched.

### The clang-cl build of GAOL gets the flags interval arithmetic needs

CMake sets `MSVC` for clang-cl, so `CMakeLists.txt` gives it what it gives
Visual C++: `/fp:strict`, `/arch:AVX2` (x64), `/FI gaol/core_math_port.h`.
`clang-cl-18 -### /fp:strict` shows `-frounding-math
-ffp-exception-behavior=strict -ffp-contract=off` (the default is
`-fno-rounding-math -ffp-contract=on`). Checked by configuring GAOL with CMake
3.28 and clang-cl-18 on Linux (`CMAKE_SYSTEM_NAME=Windows`, no link, Ninja):
"Interval arithmetic flags: /fp:strict", "Fused multiply-add flags:
/arch:AVX2", and the 36 CORE-MATH sources built by the target `gaol_core_math`
with `/O2 /Ob2 /DNDEBUG -MD /FI.../core_math_port.h /fp:strict /arch:AVX2`, no
warning. No change to `CMakeLists.txt` was needed. Other points read:

- `gaol/gaol_u128.h`: clang-cl x64 defines `__SIZEOF_INT128__` → `unsigned
  __int128`. The 36 clang-cl objects reference no `__udivti3`/`__multi3`/
  `__chkstk`: only `_fltused`, `__security_cookie`/`__security_check_cookie`,
  `sqrt`, `feraiseexcept`, `nan`, `ldexp`, `fmin`, `fmax`, `round`, the `fe*`
  flag functions, `printf`/`puts`/`exit` — all UCRT/vcruntime.

- `gaol_fpu_fenv.h`: clang-cl takes the GCC/Clang x86 branch (GNU inline asm

## Tests

- `tests/core_math.cpp`, new `rsqrt_hard_cases()` (called from `main`):
  `gaol_cr_rsqrt` in the four directions and `rsqrt(interval(x))` at the
  successors of the powers of 4, x = 4^k (1 + 2^-52), k = −511..511 (1,023
  arguments, 2 × 1,023 checks). The roundings are derived analytically:
  1/sqrt(x) = 2^-k (1 + 2^-52)^(-1/2) lies strictly between 2^-k (1 − 2^-53)
  and 2^-k, about 1.5 × 2^-53 ulp above the first, so RD = RN = RZ = 2^-k (1 −
  2^-53), RU = 2^-k (checked with mpmath at 2,000 bits on 30 of them). These
  are the arguments where `as_rsqrt_refine()` adds the last ulp only when
  `get_rounding_mode()` says upward: 1,024 of the 1,338 wrong clang-cl results
  of the emulation are these. (Corrected in the review round.) The existing
  `recommended_tightest()` sees the defect too, but only as a lack of
  tightness: it compares GAOL's bounds with CORE-MATH called downward and
  upward, which share the defect, while GAOL builds its lower bound of `rsqrt`
  and `asinpi` as the double below the upward value; with clang-cl before the
  fix, "rsqrt over an interval: the tightest bounds" fails 7,963 of 36,396
  checks and "asinpi over an interval" 16,317 of 31,190 (the review's real
  clang-cl build; both counts are part of the 26,558 below). It cannot see an
  upper bound below the exact value, which `rsqrt_hard_cases()` checks against
  values derived apart from CORE-MATH.
- The existing checks cover `cbrt` (`cbrt_hard_cases()`, point 6) and `asinpi`
  ("asinpi next to +-1": the four roundings consistent, the directed ones one
  double apart); their comments now mention clang-cl (cbrt) and
  `doc/tests.md` describes the rsqrt check.
- The CI job runs all of them with clang-cl x64, in Release and Debug.

## Behaviour change

Only with clang-cl on x86-64: `cbrt`, `rsqrt` and `asinpi` (and so
`nth_root(x, 3)`, `rootn(x, 3)`, `rsqrt`, `asinpi` of GAOL) now round in the
direction in effect; before, the directed roundings were taken for toward zero
(the wrong upward results, at positive arguments, below the exact value, the
wrong downward ones, at negative arguments, above it), and the bound of
`nth_root(x, 3)` farther from zero and the upper bounds of `rsqrt` and
`asinpi` did not enclose at hard arguments (at about half of all `asinpi`
arguments). With clang-cl before the fix, `nth_root([-0x1.3a9ccd7f022dbp+0],
3)` had `-0x1.1236160ba9b93p+0` as lower bound, above the exact value (the
review's run). No change on any other compiler (object code identical
on Linux).

## Open questions and decisions for the maintainer

1. Refuse clang-cl without `/fp:strict` too? More pressing now that clang-cl
   is a platform of the CI. clang-cl defines `_M_FP_STRICT`
   with `/fp:strict` from Clang 16 on (probed with libclang 13 to 18; 13–15
   define no `_M_FP_*`; the review confirmed with Clang 18: `-ffp-model=strict`
   defines `_M_FP_STRICT _M_FP_EXCEPT`, precise `_M_FP_PRECISE`, fast
   `_M_FP_FAST`): `gaol_config.h` could test `defined(_MSC_VER) &&
   (!defined(__clang__) || __clang_major__ >= 16)`, and `tests/fp_strict` then
   check clang-cl as well. Without `/fp:strict`, clang-cl compiles with
   `-fno-rounding-math -ffp-contract=on`.
2. Cygwin x64 is still wrong (newlib's `FE_*` are 0 to 3, neither `__WIN32__`
   nor `_WIN32`): the three functions take the directed roundings for toward
   zero there, as 3rd/README.md's patch 6 says. Take patch 6 in full (the
   table form, right whatever the libc), or refuse Cygwin in `gaol_config.h`?
   Cygwin is not in the CI.
3. Not this point's bug, not fixed: `rounding_direction` fails on
   configure-clean (`6ef2387`): #57 makes the width and center formats write
   `[empty]`, and the check "the other outputs" of
   `tests/rounding_direction.cpp` (from #50) still expects `empty`
   (`write(E(), interval_format::width) == "empty"`). Every job of the CI
   will fail on it, the new clang-cl jobs included.
4. Name clang-cl x64 in the list of compilers of the manual (§ Prerequisites)
   and of `doc/three-builds.md` once the CI is green (after open questions 3
   and 6)?
5. clang-cl of Visual Studio 2026 (image `windows-2025-vs2026`, if it has the
   component) and clang-cl x86/arm64 are not in the CI; this fix does not
   concern them (no `__x86_64__`).
6. **A new point, not fixed here (found by the review): built by clang-cl,
   `GAOL_INFINITY` is not infinite when the caller rounds downward or toward
   zero.** `gaol/gaol_port.h` defines `GAOL_INFINITY` as `HUGE_VAL`; the UCRT
   (Windows SDK 10.0.26100, `corecrt_math.h`) defines `HUGE_VAL` as
   `((double)INFINITY)` and `INFINITY` as `((float)(_HUGE_ENUF))`, `_HUGE_ENUF`
   being `1e+300` (`(float)(1e300*1e300)` under `_UCRT_LEGACY_INFINITY`).
   Under `/fp:strict` clang-cl does not fold that inexact conversion: it
   emits `vcvtsd2ss` + `vcvtss2sd` at run time (seen here in the object of
   `double f(void) { return HUGE_VAL; }`, clang-cl 18 with the SDK's headers;
   `/fp:precise` folds it to a constant). Run under wine, `HUGE_VAL` is then
   `0x1.fffffep+127` (FLT_MAX) rounding downward or toward zero, and raises
   overflow and inexact in all four directions. `GAOL_INFINITY` appears in
   inline code of the public headers too (`gaol_interval.h`,
   `gaol_interval_fpu.h`...), so the code using GAOL is concerned as well.
   Effects, while the caller rounds downward or toward zero: `interval()` and
   `interval(1.0) / interval(-1.0, 1.0)` are `[-0x1.fffffep+127,
   0x1.fffffep+127]` (the review, GAOL built by clang-cl 18 under wine), `pow`
   gives `[-nan, nan]`, and the test of `interval(double)` (`-GAOL_INFINITY <
   a && a < GAOL_INFINITY`) is false for 1e300, which makes `interval(1e300)`
   the empty set (that test compiled by clang-cl 18, run under wine,
   `tmp/47/hv/`). `rounding_direction` already has teeth for it: 37 failed
   checks with clang-cl, none with `HUGE_VAL` forced to
   `__builtin_huge_val()`. Visual C++, GCC, Clang and MinGW are not concerned
   (Visual C++ folds the conversion, as the review says, and its jobs passed
